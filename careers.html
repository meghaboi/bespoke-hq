01 — Why join

Freelancing broke because the incentives broke. We rebuilt the parts that matter: scoped work, named leads, and money that arrives on time.

01

Guaranteed milestone pay

Every sprint has written deliverables and a fixed fee. Hit the milestone, get paid on release. No chasing, no 90-day terms.

02

Zero unpaid pitches

We win the client before you see the brief. You never build a spec deck, a free mockup or a test edit to compete for work.

03

Direct project channels

One channel, one assigned project lead, one source of truth. Feedback is consolidated before it reaches you, not forwarded raw.

04

Remote and autonomous

Work from anywhere on a sprint schedule you accept in advance. We care about the delivery date, not your calendar colour.

What we ask in return

Craft you would put your own name on, honest communication when something slips, and files delivered the way the brief asked for them. Directors review every deliverable before it reaches a client, so the standard is the standard.

Hit that consistently and you move up the roster: first pick of sprints, larger scopes, and retained monthly work instead of one-offs.

03 — How to apply

Three steps, forty-eight hours.

No multi-round interview process for contract work. We look at what you have made and we make a call.

01

Send the two-minute application

Name, role, location and links. Portfolio, showreel, GitHub, Instagram, whatever shows the work best. Skip the cover letter. We would rather see three real projects than a paragraph about your passion.

2 min
02

We review within 48 hours

A project director reviews every application personally. If the work fits, you get a short call to talk rates, availability and the kind of sprints you want. If it does not, you get a straight answer instead of silence.

48 hrs
03

Get your Creative Portal access

You are onboarded to the portal, added to the roster for your discipline, and start receiving sprint offers with scope, deadline and fee attached. Accept the ones that work for you.

Then ongoing

04 — Apply

Two minutes. Then we do the rest.

Applications stay open year-round, even when no sprint is live for your discipline. We keep good people on file and come back when the work lands.

By applying you agree to be contacted about project work. We do not sell or share your details, and you can ask to be removed from the roster at any time.
